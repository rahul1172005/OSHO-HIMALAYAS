Methodology & Philosophy

Why OSHO Active Meditations?

Traditional silent meditation was created thousands of years ago for people living in simple, pastoral, and unhurried societies. The modern person carries deep nervous system stress and overstimulated intellect. Asking a restless mind to sit still immediately produces tension. Active meditation clears the vessel first.

Step 1

Active Breath

Fast, chaotic breathing awakens dormant physical energy and breaks habitual holding patterns.

Step 2

Catharsis

Safe, uninhibited release of repressed emotions, stress, and somatic tension through movement and sound.

Step 3

Dance & Rhythm

Celebratory movement integrates physical awareness and reconnects the bodily center of joy.

Step 4

Effortless Stillness

With the body cleansed and energy harmonized, silence and witness consciousness arrive without effort.

Travel Directions

How to Reach OSHO Himalayas

Nestled near Dharamshala in the Kangra District of Himachal Pradesh, our retreat is easily accessible by air, road, and high-speed rail.

By Air

45 Minutes from Kangra Airport

Daily direct flights operate from New Delhi (DEL) to Dharamshala / Kangra Airport (DHM). Our reservation desk coordinates pre-arranged taxi transfers directly to the sanctuary gates.

By Train

Vande Bharat Express Route

Board the high-speed Vande Bharat Express from New Delhi to Amb Andaura station. From the station, enjoy a scenic 2-hour taxi journey through gentle Himalayan pine slopes.

By Luxury Bus

Overnight Volvo Express

Comfortable semi-sleeper Volvo buses depart daily from New Delhi (ISBT Kashmere Gate or Majnu-ka-Tilla) directly to Dharamshala. Arrive refreshed in the morning mountain air.

Sanctuary Cab Desk

Direct Door-to-Door Service

Let us know your travel schedule when reserving. Our dedicated transport team arranges verified local drivers for airport, railway station, or intercity pickups at standard rates.
